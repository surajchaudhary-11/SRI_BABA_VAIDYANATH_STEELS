How to choose TMT grade for your house

Published for builders and homeowners in Kanpur.

Start with structural design

Grade selection must follow your structural engineer's design, not just price. For most residential projects, Fe500D is preferred because it balances strength and ductility.

Why Fe500D is common

The "D" grade has better elongation performance than regular Fe500. This improves energy absorption under dynamic loads and reduces brittle failure risk.

When Fe550D is used

Fe550D can reduce steel quantity in select designs but must be used only when the design assumptions and site execution quality are reliable.

Checklist before buying

1. Verify IS 1786 marking

Ensure brand embossing and grade marks are visible on bars.

2. Ask for test certificate

Check yield strength, UTS, elongation, and bend/rebend values.

3. Confirm weight tolerance

Procure from trusted channel partners with weighbridge transparency.
