Difference between HR and CR steel sheets

A practical guide for procurement teams and fabricators.

Manufacturing route

HR sheets are rolled at high temperature. CR sheets are further processed at near-room temperature for tighter thickness and better finish.

Surface and tolerance

HR: rougher surface, wider tolerance, economical for general fabrication. CR: smoother surface, tighter tolerance, preferred for precision and painting.

Typical applications

HR Sheet

Base frames, industrial structures, heavy fabrication, and components where finish is secondary.

CR Sheet

Automotive panels, appliance bodies, cabinets, ducts and products needing better dimensional control.

Price factors

CR generally costs more due to additional processing. Choose based on application need, not just per-ton comparison.
